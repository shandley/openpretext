# Superseded manuscript drafts (archived 2026-07-13)

These are the two paper drafts from the original (early 2026) publication plan.
They are kept here for reference only. They do not reflect the current plan.

- `paper1-nar-platform.md` — the large platform/software paper (filename targets
  Nucleic Acids Research; later notes drifted toward Genome Biology with a P(s)
  R-squared biological-insight angle). Framing predates most of OpenPretext's
  current feature set.
- `paper2-autosort-autocut.md` — the short AutoSort/AutoCut methods paper.
- `references-paper1.bib`, `references-paper2.bib` — their bibliographies.

## Why archived

OpenPretext evolved substantially after these were written, and the plans no
longer match the software. On 2026-07-13 we chose to re-evaluate publication
strategy from scratch, deliberately setting aside the old findings and framing
so a fresh capability review and a current survey of the field could drive the
new plan rather than the old conclusions anchoring it.

The raw cross-species analysis data in `manuscript/analysis/` was left in place;
it is data, not narrative, and a fresh plan may or may not use it.
