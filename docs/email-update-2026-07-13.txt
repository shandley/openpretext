Hi Belen and Carlos,

A short update on OpenPretext.

The main new thing is a set of practice genomes from across the tree of life (a starfish, an earthworm, a mushroom, a plant, and more), each with a brief guided exercise where you curate a real, uncurated assembly yourself. They sit alongside the bird and vertebrate examples, and everything loads faster now.

A few smaller items you may find useful:
- A haplotig detector flags likely duplicate-haplotype contigs, which should help on fragmented assemblies.
- Live quality stats (scaffold N50, auN, percent assigned) update as you curate, with a before/after view of the map.
- For the 3D side, the analysis panel gained an observed/expected map toggle and a P(s) decay curve.

Everything is live at https://shandley.github.io/openpretext/, with a plain-language guide at https://openpretext-guide.vercel.app.

I would value your feedback, especially on the new exercises. No rush.
